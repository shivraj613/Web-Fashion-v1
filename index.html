What We Do ?

12+ Years of Experience

We provide structural concrete form work engineering and form work design services for all kinds of big and small scale construction projects through out the India. Our proficiency in Formwork solution includes the engineering of proprietary systems, custom designed systems and job built systems. Specific applications that we expert in include the design of formwork for monolithic construction, stair cores, elevator cores, walls, one sided walls, slabs, beams, girders, and other structures. We have expertise with all the key form work manufacturers through out the world.

Our Products

VABA Aluminium Formwork

VABA is a leading manufacturer of aluminum formwork systems in India. The company's products are used in a wide variety of construction projects, including high-rise buildings, bridges, and tunnels. VABA's aluminum formwork systems offer a number of advantages over traditional steel formwork systems, including:

  • Lighter weight, which makes them easier to handle and transport
  • More durable, with a longer lifespan
  • More recyclable, which is better for the environment
  • More versatile, as they can be used for a wider range of applications

Features and Benefits

VABA's aluminum formwork systems are made from high-quality aluminum alloy 6061-T6, which is known for its strength, durability, and corrosion resistance. The systems are also designed to be lightweight and easy to assemble, making them ideal for use in tall buildings and other projects with tight deadlines.

In addition to their lightweight construction, VABA's aluminum formwork systems also offer a number of other benefits, including:

  • High strength-to-weight ratio
  • Excellent dimensional stability
  • Smooth surface finish
  • Easy to clean and maintain
  • Recyclable
